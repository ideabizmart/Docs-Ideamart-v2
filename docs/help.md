## What can we help you with?

- **Developer support:** API documentation, authentication, integration, and error troubleshooting.
- **Application onboarding:** Registration, provisioning, review, and production activation.
- **Service provider support:** Questions about your applications, subscriptions, and service operations.
- **Business inquiries:** Partnerships and opportunities within the Ideamart ecosystem.

## When contacting support

For a faster response, include the following information where relevant:

1. Your name and organization or service provider name.
2. Your application ID (if applicable).
3. A short description of your question or issue.
4. The relevant API, error code, and timestamp (for technical issues).
5. Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.

**Security:** Never share passwords, API secrets, OTPs, or other sensitive credentials in support messages.

## Ideamart for Women (IFW)

**Ideamart for Women (IFW)** is an initiative of Ideamart that aims to reduce the gender gap in Sri Lanka's developer ecosystem and create opportunities for more women to become technology entrepreneurs and founders.

The programme supports women in transforming ideas into digital products and businesses through connections, practical guidance, learning opportunities, mentorship, and access to the Ideamart ecosystem. It welcomes female founders at different stages, from exploring their first idea to launching and growing a service.

**Learn more:** [Ideamart for Women on the official Ideamart website](https://www.ideamart.lk/) (or select **Ideamart for Women** from the website menu).

### Who can get involved?

- **Female tech entrepreneurs:** Women exploring, developing, launching, or growing technology-based ideas and businesses.
- **Universities and tech societies:** Groups interested in collaborating on workshops, learning sessions, and community activities.
- **Women's networks and community organisations:** Partners seeking to expand participation and opportunities for women in technology.

### The IFW empowerment journey

1. **Discover:** Explore digital tools, mobile network APIs, and no-code opportunities.
2. **Collaborate:** Connect with people and communities through programme activities.
3. **Build:** Develop a working digital service with practical guidance.
4. **Launch:** Bring the service to market and reach customers.
5. **Make an impact:** Grow as a founder and contribute to Sri Lanka's women-led technology community.

### IFW community activities

The programme's official webpage highlights activities and community stories including:

- **International Women in Engineering (WIE) Day:** A gathering at the Dialog Auditorium on 26 July 2025 celebrating women in STEM.
- **She Means Business:** A full-day workshop focused on helping female entrepreneurs develop ideas into ventures.
- **Community learning and collaboration:** Opportunities to connect, learn about digital tools, and gain confidence in creating services.
